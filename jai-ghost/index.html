JAI GHOST

Jai Loyal, also known as Jai Ghost — pronounced “Jay Ghost” — is a music artist, filmmaker and visual storyteller. Jai Ghost World brings the music, cinematic visuals, live performance identity, GHOST-WEAR and the wider story together in one place.

MUSIC + FILM

The sound moves across hip-hop, rap, rock and heavier rap-metal / trap-metal territory. The visual side is built with the same intent: music videos, cinematic storytelling, behind-the-scenes filmmaking and live performance are treated as parts of one connected world.
